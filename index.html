Course Content

Nine Modules. From Prompts to Orchestration.

Each module builds on the last, so you move from writing clear instructions to running multi-agent workflows on real projects.

Understand LLMs, AI agents, and agentic workflows. Learn where AI helps and when human judgment is essential.

Write clear instructions with defined goals, constraints, examples, and expected outputs.

Provide the documents, background, and reference material an agent needs to work effectively.

Set up the tools, understand their interfaces, and practise workflows suited to each.

Understand how instructions, tools, memory, permissions, and checks help an AI model complete useful work.

Break a process into steps. Connect supported tools, handle failures, and review outputs before important actions.

Assign different tasks to appropriate agents and bring their outputs into a shared workflow.

Build, test, document, and demonstrate projects that address personal or business needs.

Explore how to use your projects in your current role, freelance services, or business operations.
